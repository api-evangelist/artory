# Winston Artory Group

> Winston Artory Group is an expert-led art appraisal, advisory, collection management, investment advisory, and art data platform serving private collectors, estates, fiduciaries, legal professionals, financial institutions, family offices, museums, and institutions.

Winston Artory Group combines specialist art expertise, market intelligence, secure digital systems, and structured collection data to help clients evaluate, acquire, manage, protect, and sell art and collectibles.

## Services

- [Expert Appraisals](https://www.wag-art.com/services/expert-appraisals): USPAP-compliant valuations for art and collectibles. Appraisal use cases include insurance, estate planning, estate tax, charitable donation, equitable distribution, damage or loss, collateralized lending, strategic sales, and family division.
- [Advisory & Collection Management](https://www.wag-art.com/services/advisory-collection-management): Strategic guidance for acquiring, selling, documenting, managing, preserving, and deaccessioning art and collectible collections.
- [Investment Management](https://www.wag-art.com/services/investment-management): Art investment advisory and management for collectors, private investors, family offices, and institutions.
- [Collection Data Service](https://www.wag-art.com/services/collection-data-service): Collection cataloging, structured artwork records, valuation data, condition information, provenance documentation, digital collection records, and reporting.

## Collection Data and Technology

WAG’s collection data services help clients structure, organize, and maintain digital records for art and collectibles. These records may include artist, title, date, medium, dimensions, provenance, exhibition history, literature, acquisition details, condition, location, insurance value, fair market value, retail replacement value, and supporting documentation.

Collection data supports valuation, insurance, estate planning, fiduciary review, reporting, collection management, and strategic decision-making.
